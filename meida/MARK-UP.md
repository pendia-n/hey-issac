# Meida: Product Summary

Updated: 3 November 2026.

## In One Paragraph

Meida is a subscription app that turns a business website into a visual market room. A user submits a website, gets a themed room with a 3D character, follows sourced updates about the business and selected competitors, and asks questions or drafts marketing wording. The aim is to make keeping up with a market feel more approachable and enjoyable. The character presents real work and findings; the user remains responsible for deciding what to do and publishing any content.

App: https://meida.pendia-community.workers.dev/

Visual preview: https://meida.pendia-community.workers.dev/hinneh

## Who It Is For

Meida is intended for solo business owners, makers, creators and small teams who want a place to revisit what is happening around their business without repeatedly searching across many websites. Higher plans accommodate several businesses or a small portfolio.

The current workflow starts with a public website URL. It is not a general text-prompt service for monitoring any person or topic.

## What Makes The Experience Different

The visual room is central to the product. Meida should feel like visiting a small creative space that follows a business, rather than opening a dense marketing dashboard. Its practical value comes from real sources, competitor information, website observations and conversation.

The character has three prepared appearances: a base state for resting, a working state while a task runs, and a responding state when a result is ready. The app switches between those prepared poses. It does not use a fully animated human performing arbitrary movements.

There are eight character and setting categories:

| Category | Setting |
| --- | --- |
| Art and painting | Artist's studio |
| Music | Piano rehearsal room |
| Food | Restaurant kitchen |
| Agriculture | Farm and ranch |
| Clothing | Fashion atelier and fitting room |
| Gaming and esports | Esports arena |
| Tourism | Outdoor coastal visitor promenade |
| Commercial and other businesses | Office |

The commercial character is the fallback when a website does not fit another category. Characters and their prepared poses are reusable assets, not newly generated likenesses for each submitted business. Competitors appear in a panel beside the viewer; they do not require additional 3D characters.

## What A User Does

1. View the public visual preview before purchasing. It demonstrates the rooms and poses without presenting invented market findings.
2. Create an account and choose a monthly plan before adding a website.
3. Submit a website URL to create a room. The currently enabled creation flow performs an initial search and a basic check of that page.
4. Use Refresh overview for a fuller business description, and Find competitors to obtain candidates with descriptions and evidence. Confirm which competitors to keep.
5. Revisit the room for scheduled updates, inspect the original sources, or use Check now within the plan allowance.
6. Open Website check for page observations and suggested improvements, or Ask the room for questions and marketing drafts.

One website allowance represents one root website domain. Different product pages on the same domain do not become separate rooms. The submitted page is retained for its page check. The user may monitor a business they own or another public business; submitting a URL does not establish ownership of that business.

## What Users Get

| Capability | What it provides |
| --- | --- |
| Business overview | A saved description of the offering, audience and business model when an overview refresh succeeds. |
| Competitor discovery | Suggested competing businesses, descriptions and source links for the user to assess and select. |
| Market updates | Saved public mentions and official announcements with source links and available dates. |
| Competitor updates | Weekly searches about each selected competitor, with findings shown in the room's update feed. |
| Website check | Observations about the submitted page's title, description, heading, canonical address and indexing instructions. Newer reviews also retain evidence and review history. |
| Improvement suggestions | AI-generated recommendations grounded in the page observations when a manual review and its advice generation succeed. |
| Questions and drafts | Replies using public sources, relevant saved findings and recent conversation. Users can request suggested marketing wording and social media posts. |
| Partner investigation | An explicitly requested deeper market investigation, limited to one per account per month. |

A website check is an on-page review. It does not establish search rankings, traffic, backlinks, search volumes, whole-site health or visibility across AI answer services. Meida currently has no verified numerical SEO or GEO rating.

News coverage depends on what the searches return. A successful check can find nothing new. Repeated source URLs are filtered, but the app does not guarantee that different articles about the same event will always be recognised as one story. Publication dates and discovery dates are distinct; some sources have no usable publication date.

## Monthly Subscriptions

Prices are in US dollars. The current product has monthly subscriptions, not weekly subscriptions or a separate $9 first-visit purchase.

| Included allowance | Solo | Studio | Partner |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Monthly price | $19 | $49 | $129 |
| Active websites | 1 | 3 | 8 |
| Competitors per website | Up to 3 | Up to 5 | Up to 8 |
| Main-site checks per website per week | 6 | 12 | 18 |
| Approximate main-site checks per website per month | 26 | 52 | 78 |
| Questions and drafting turns per account per month | 30 | 150 | 400 |
| Extra manual searches per account per month | 2 | 6 | 20 |
| Scheduled website reviews per website per month | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| Additional manual website reviews per website per month | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| Manual overview refresh per website per month | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Deeper market investigation per account per month | None | None | 1 |
| Make individual rooms private | No | No | Yes |

Questions, drafting turns and extra manual searches are shared across all websites in the account. The manual search allowance covers both Check now and fresh competitor discovery. Competitor limits apply separately to each website. Usage allowances currently count by calendar month rather than necessarily matching the subscription renewal date.

## When Updates Happen

All scheduled times below use New York time and follow its daylight-saving changes.

| Plan | Main-site market checks |
| --- | --- |
| Solo | Sunday through Friday at 1pm |
| Studio | Sunday through Friday at 9am and 1pm |
| Partner | Sunday through Thursday at 9am, 1pm and 9pm; Friday at 9am, 1pm and 3pm |

There are no regular Saturday main-site market checks. Each selected competitor is searched once per week, assigned to a day from Sunday through Friday at 2pm. Competitor monitoring is separate from the main-site checks above.

Scheduled website reviews occur on the 19th at 9pm for Solo and Studio, and on the 11th and 22nd at 9pm for Partner. Work can be delayed or fail; these are scheduled checks, not a promise of continuous monitoring or an exact delivery time. Failed scheduled tasks have bounded retries.

Opening the room does not itself launch another paid web search. Users can read saved findings between checks. A resting character does not mean that new research has occurred.

## Sharing, Accounts And Billing

Rooms are public by default. Public visitors can see the character room and saved public updates. The competitor controls, website review and owner conversation are available to the account owner. Partner members can make a room private.

Accounts use a username and password. The current version does not provide self-service password recovery after losing access. Members can manage their profile, password and billing after signing in.

The current billing flow supports cancellation and offers a 50% refund of the first successful subscription payment when requested within 47 hours; that request also ends the subscription immediately. Renewals are not included in that refund offer. To change plans, the current subscription must end before another is selected.

## Prepared But Disabled

The last confirmed release left the additional automatic research features disabled. These include a richer business overview and competitor discovery during room creation, recurring business-overview refreshes, recurring competitor rediscovery, and AI-written advice after scheduled website reviews. Their manual counterparts are implemented.

The owner questioned the addition of recurring business reviews. They should not be treated as an accepted product requirement or enabled merely because the code exists. Scheduled market searches, weekly competitor monitoring and basic scheduled page reviews are separate from these disabled additions.

## Product Boundaries

Meida suggests social media posts; it does not publish to Reddit, X or other platforms. It does not automatically edit a customer's website, generate a unique character for every competitor, guarantee new findings at every visit, or guarantee marketing results. Default, Max and Push model modes and usage add-on purchases are not part of the current implemented offer.

The current product is a reusable 3D room with prepared character poses, sourced market updates and bounded research interactions. Earlier ideas involving a large animated 2D office, rival character groups, a separate paid first visit or different subscription prices should not be mistaken for the current Meida offer.
